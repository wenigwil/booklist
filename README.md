# Booklist

The booklist makes no attempt to be complete or even a collection of *good* books. It 
just tries to give more options to search instead of searching on the web or through 
a chat bot.

## Springer Series Solid State Physics

Judging from the title only, I found the very general works

- **The Structure and Properties of Matter** *Matsubara*
- **Computational Materials Science** *Ohno et. al.*
- **Semiconductor Physics** *Seeger*
- **Strongly Correlated Systems** *Avella*

### Symmetry or Group Theory

- **Mirror Symmetry** *Wohab*
- **Group Theory And Its Applications in Physics** *Inui, Tanabe, Onodeva*
- **Symmetries in Physics** *Ludwig, Falter*

### Many-Body Methods

- **Green's Functions in Quantum Physics** *Economou*
- **Many-Body Approach to Electronic Excitations** *Bechsted*
- **Quantum Solid-State Physics** *Vonsovsky*
- **Field Theories for Low-Dimensional Condensed Matter Systems** *Morandi et. al.*
- **Electron Scattering in Solid Matter** *Weinberger et. al.*

### Transport

- **Quantum Theory of Magnetism** *White*
- **Quantum Transport in Submicron Devices** *Magnus, Schoenmaker*
- **Theory of Electron Transport in Semiconductors** *Jacobini*
- **Theory of Quantum Transport at Nanoscale** *Ryndyk*
- **Localization, Interaction and Transport Phenomena** *Kramer, Bergmann, 
  Bruynseraede*

### Statistical Physics

- **Statistical Physics I** *Kubo et. al.*
- **Statistical Physics II** *Kubo et. al.*
- **Monte Carlo Simulation in Statistical Physics** *Binder, Heermann*
- **Physics of Entropy** *Miyashita*
